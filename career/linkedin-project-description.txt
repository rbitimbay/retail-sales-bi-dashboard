PROJECT NAME
Retail Sales Business Intelligence Dashboard

ASSOCIATED WITH
Astana IT University

DESCRIPTION
Developed a Retail Sales Business Intelligence Dashboard in Microsoft Power BI as part of a two-person project.

Analyzed transactional retail data across products, categories, cities, and time periods. Created DAX measures for key business KPIs including Total Sales, Number of Orders, and Average Order Value.

Implemented descriptive statistics, interactive filtering, Decomposition Tree analysis, Market Basket Analysis, geographic sales analysis, and Power BI Q&A.

The project demonstrates the use of Business Intelligence techniques to transform transactional data into insights that can support product, marketing, and regional business decisions.

Technologies: Power BI, DAX, Business Intelligence, Data Analysis, Data Visualization.

FEATURED SECTION TITLE
Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard — Power BI

FEATURED SECTION DESCRIPTION
Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing retail sales performance, product trends, purchasing behavior, and geographic sales distribution.
